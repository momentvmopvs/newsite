Gold Max and Gold Fosco Vinyl Imprimax

Imprimax's Gold Max and Gold Fosco lines are solid color adhesive films in gloss, semi-gloss, matte and pearl finishes. Most colors have a Pantone reference, which makes it easier to match brand colors.

They are used for indoor and outdoor signage, booths, fleet graphics (partial or full wraps), facades, walls and furniture. Use the filters to find a color and click it to enlarge and request a quote.
