Gold Madeiras Wood Vinyl Imprimax

Imprimax's Gold Madeiras line brings self-adhesive vinyl with realistic wood prints: from light and greyish tones to dark woods, rustic reclaimed wood and slats. It is one of the most popular ways to refresh furniture, doors, panels and walls without construction work.

The material is high-quality PVC with anti-mold and antibacterial treatment, and it is flame retardant. Click any color to enlarge it, see the code and request a quote with it.
