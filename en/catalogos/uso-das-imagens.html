Use of catalogue images

The color images in our virtual catalogues are here to help you choose the finish for your project. They are not for sale and are not licensed for use outside this site.

Each image has a different owner. See below who owns it and who to contact if you want to use it elsewhere.

Who owns the images

Alltak

The images of the Alto Brilho and Kroma lines come from the Alltak catalogue and belong to Alltak. To use them outside this site, ask Alltak for permission directly.

Imprimax

The images of the Gold Madeiras, Gold Max e Gold Fosco, Gold Pedras, Pastilhas Gold, Pisomax and Max Lux lines come from the Imprimax catalogue and belong to Imprimax. To use them outside this site, ask Imprimax for permission directly.

Momentvm Opvs

The digital samples in the ACM panel catalogue were drawn by Momentvm Opvs. To use them outside this site, get in touch with us.

On-screen colors are a reference. Before closing the project, compare them with the physical sample: we bring it to you.
