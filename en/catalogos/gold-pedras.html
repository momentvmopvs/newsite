Gold Pedras Stone Vinyl Imprimax

Imprimax's Gold Pedras line reproduces stone surfaces with realistic prints: marbles (Carrara, Calacatta, Nero, Travertine), burnt cement, slate, terrazzo, bricks and 3D cubes. It is a fast way to change the look of countertops, walls and furniture without demolition.

The vinyl is suitable for indoor and outdoor use, has a washable surface (household cleaners are fine), is flame retardant and mold resistant. Click any pattern to enlarge it and request a quote with it.
