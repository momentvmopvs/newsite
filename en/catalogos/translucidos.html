Max Lux Translucent Vinyl Imprimax

Imprimax's Max Lux line brings translucent calendered vinyl: it lets light through and keeps the color even, which makes it the right choice for illuminated signs, backlit letters, shop windows and glass.

The material bonds well to acrylic and glass and keeps color fidelity when the sign is on. Click any color to enlarge it and request a quote with it.
