Ultra High Gloss Vinyl Alltak

Alltak's Ultra line is a super-calendered polymeric PVC film with high gloss, in solid, metallic, color shift (Illusion) and glitter (Kosmic) colors. The result is a mirror-like finish that looks like automotive paint.

It is one of the most popular choices to wrap furniture, cabinet doors, appliances and vehicles. Click any color to enlarge it, see the code and request a quote with it.
