Gold Mosaic Tile Vinyl Imprimax

Imprimax's Gold Pastilhas line is a self-adhesive vinyl that looks like mosaic tiles, with a realistic printed relief. It is a quick way to refresh kitchen, bathroom, laundry and commercial walls without removing the existing tiles.

The PVC has anti-mold and antibacterial treatment and is flame retardant. It lasts up to 4 years outdoors and up to 8 years indoors. Click any pattern to enlarge it and request a quote with it.
