Kroma Adhesive Covering Alltak Decor

Alltak Decor's Kroma line is an adhesive covering with a textured matte finish, in solid colors (Kroma Forma), woods (Kroma Wood) and rattan and leather textures. Its super-permanent acrylic adhesive holds well even on uneven surfaces.

The calendered polymeric film (140 microns, 1.22 m wide) installs cleanly and without odor, is anti-mold and anti-fungal, flame retardant and resistant to abrasive products. It lasts 7 years indoors. Click any color to enlarge it and request a quote with it.
