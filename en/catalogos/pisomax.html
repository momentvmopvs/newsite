Pisomax Floor Vinyl Imprimax

Imprimax's Pisomax line is an adhesive floor film: it refreshes a space without breaking or removing the existing floor and tiles. There are realistic patterns (burnt cement, wood and marble) and anti-slip versions.

It is meant for low-traffic areas such as offices, bedrooms, booths and stores with light foot traffic. Click any option to enlarge it and request a quote with it.
