ACM Colors for Facades Chapas AlumBrasil

ACM (aluminium composite material) is the most used cladding for commercial facades, pylons, gateways and signs: light, rigid, weather resistant and easy to clean. We work with AlumBrasil panels, 3 and 4 mm thick, in 1220 × 5000 and 1500 × 5000 mm sheets.

Here are the most requested colors and finishes: matte, high gloss, metallic, brushed, mirror and wood. Click any color to enlarge it and request a quote with it. If the color you need is not listed, talk to us: we source it from other manufacturers.
