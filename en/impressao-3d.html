3D Printing for Visual Communication

Momentvm Opvs produces 3D printed letters, logos and parts using filament printing, with the same attention to finishing we bring to our signage and set design projects.

3D Letters and Logos

Channel letters, raised logos and brand identity elements produced from your brand files. 3D printing allows volumes, curves and details that are hard to achieve with cut sheets, with no mold required. Scale models, prototypes and custom parts are evaluated case by case.

Materials and Sizes

We work with PLA, PETG and ABS, chosen according to where the piece will be installed and the finish required. Each part can be up to 40 × 40 × 40 cm; larger projects are printed in sections and joined into a single piece.

Finishing and Painting

After printing, parts can be sanded, joined and painted for a uniform surface. That is how the large-scale bus model in the photo above was made: printed in sections, joined and painted.
