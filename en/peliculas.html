Insulfilm Window Films and Frosted Glass

Momentvm Opvs specializes in the application of Insulfilm window films in São Paulo, offering functional and elegant solutions for residential, commercial and corporate environments. We work with high-performance materials to provide thermal comfort, safety and privacy without compromising aesthetics. All of our Insulfilm window films are manufactured by Nexfill USA, a major American company in the architectural film industry.

Solar Control Window Films for Glass and Windows

Protect your home or business with safety window films, which increase resistance to impacts and prevent shattering in case of breakage. They are recommended for schools, retail spaces, condominiums, clinics and any location where personal and property safety is a priority.

Frosted Films for Privacy and Design

Frosted and decorative films bring sophistication and privacy to glass partitions, doors and windows. Widely used in offices, clinics and corporate environments, they can be customized with a logo, stripes or an exclusive design, aligning visual communication with functionality.

Technical Application with a Professional Finish

Correct installation of the films is essential to ensure adhesion, durability and a clean finish on the glass. Momentvm Opvs evaluates the type of environment, the purpose of the application and the surface characteristics to recommend the most suitable solution for each project.
