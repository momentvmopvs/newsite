Facades and Visual Communication


We produce facades in ACM, channel letters, vinyl banners, PVC, acrylic, stainless steel, LED lighting, among others. We also develop custom projects.

ACM (Aluminum Composite Material) is a material composed of two aluminum sheets with a polyethylene core. It is resistant, durable, and offers excellent aesthetic finishing, widely used in modern facades.
