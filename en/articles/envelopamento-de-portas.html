Furniture Wrapping: Smart Renovation Without Demolition.

Are you an architect, decorator, or interior designer looking for creative solutions to renovate spaces without exceeding your client’s budget? Furniture wrapping may be exactly what you need to incorporate into your next projects.


See why wrapping is a trend among interior professionals.

With the growing demand for fast, clean, and sustainable renovations, furniture wrapping has become a modern and efficient solution. Instead of replacing all the cabinetry — which involves time, mess, and high costs — wrapping allows you to give existing furniture a new look without removing or destroying anything.


5 benefits that make wrapping the choice of demanding professionals.

  • Project budget savings

    • Wrapping is, on average, up to 95% cheaper than replacing custom-made furniture and up to 70% cheaper than refurbishing (not to mention the hassle of dismantling and reassembling furniture). These savings can be redirected to other project details, such as lighting or décor.

  • Renovation without construction work

    • No debris, no noise, and no dust. Wrapping eliminates demolition, making it ideal for residential, commercial, or corporate environments that cannot afford downtime.

  • Variety of finishes and textures

    • You can transform a simple kitchen into a sophisticated space with vinyl films that mimic marble, wood, concrete, glossy lacquer, and more. Any style or creative brief can be achieved.

  • Sustainability in practice

    • Reusing existing furniture reduces waste and the environmental impact of the project. Conscious design is on the rise — and wrapping instead of discarding is a highly valued approach.

  • Speed and practicality

    • While custom carpentry may take weeks to deliver, wrapping is completed in just a few hours, without affecting the client’s daily routine.

When should you use furniture wrapping in your projects?

  • Furniture in good structural condition but with an outdated appearance
  • Commercial spaces that need a quick refresh
  • Projects with a tight budget, without sacrificing aesthetics
  • Renovations that cannot generate debris or interrupt activities
